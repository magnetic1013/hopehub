  <p>Because I've lived it. And I still do.</p>

<div class="about-content">
  <p>Working in the field of taking calls, updating info, dealing with nut cases, and working for many institutions — I know the story.</p>
  
  <p>Not the textbook version. Not the corporate training video. The real one. The one where you're the first voice someone hears when they're frustrated, confused, or just done with everything. The one where you're juggling three spreadsheets, a ringing phone, and a manager who thinks you're "just answering phones."</p>
  
  <p>Over two decades in the public and private sectors. Seventeen years in state government. I've been in-office, hybrid, and fully remote. I've seen how each one has its own kind of hard. And I've learned that nobody talks about it honestly.</p>
  
  <p>So I started The Admin Aisle.</p>
  
  <p>Because administrative professionals are the lifeline of every business. And lifelines need somewhere to go when they're running on empty.</p>
</div>
